How to Use the Shift Report

  1. Upload Line Transactions: Select your "Line Transaction" CSV file. This contains all itemized sales, discounts, and voids.
  2. Upload Payments: Select your "Payments" CSV file.
  3. Filter Data:
    • By Staff: Isolate a specific person's performance.
    • By Method: Isolate specific payment types (e.g., Cash, Visa) to cross-reference exactly what was sold vs. how it was paid.
  4. Analyze Data:
    • Sales Table: View "Own Sales" vs "Made by Others".
    • Payments Table: See exact payments collected. Note: Transitory accounts (OPEN, CLOSE, COMP) are excluded from the "Own Payments" calculation.
